Centuries of Devotion

The Art of Indian Handloom

Every millimeter of handwoven cloth passes through 5 sacred stages of artisanal reverence, untouched by industrial machinery.

01. Cotton Carding &
Spinning

Charkha
Spinning

Indigenous Desi cotton fibers are carded by hand and spun on wooden spinning wheels into smooth, breathable yarn.

02. Warp
Preparation

Bobbin
Winding

Spun hanks are wound onto bamboo reels, sized with natural rice starch paste, and stretched outdoors for warp setting.

03. Botanical
Alchemy

Natural
Dyeing

Pure vat fermentation using wild indigo plants, madder roots, pomegranate rinds, and iron rust liquor.

04. The Rhythm of
Shuttles

Pit Loom
Weaving

The weaver sits at floor pit looms, using wooden treadles and flying shuttles to intertwine warp and weft with melodic precision.

05. Heirloom
Finishing

Knotting &
Care

Cloth is washed in sweet natural waters, hand-ironed with brass presses, and finished with artisanal fringe knotting.
