Clarity & Assurance

Frequently Asked Questions

Care instructions, shipping logistics, authenticity guarantees, and return policies.

We dispatch all ready-to-wear garments, home furnishings, and sarees within 24 to 48 business hours via insured courier. Deliveries across metropolitan India typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days. Complimentary insured delivery applies to all domestic orders over ₹1,999. International express transit to the US, UK, and UAE takes 5 to 8 business days.
We offer a hassle-free 7-day return and exchange window for unworn items with security seals and GI tags intact. Fabric yardage cut by the metre and custom-stitched sarees with stitched blouses cannot be refunded unless a physical transit flaw is verified.
Pure silk sarees and brocades should always be dry-cleaned. Khadi cotton garments and bed linens can be gently hand-washed in cold water with mild reetha or eco-detergent. Never bleach. Dry in the shade to preserve botanical indigo and madder hues. Store sarees wrapped in unbleached mulmul cloth rather than plastic.
Every saree and heirloom garment is issued with an individual serial-numbered certificate issued in collaboration with the respective state Weaver Co-operative and the Ministry of Textiles GI Registry. The QR code on the tag lets you verify the weaver community and loom registration.
