Chanderi Weaves

Silk-Cotton Sheer Translucence & Zari Butis

Known as 'woven air', Chanderi combines raw un-degummed silk warp with fine combed cotton weft, yielding a lightweight fabric beloved for tunics and summer drapes.

The Slow Craft Chronicle

The Journey From Yarn to Finished Textile

In an era of mass algorithmic fashion, discover the contemplative rhythm of Indian pit looms.

Phase 01

Fiber & Hank
Preparation

Pure raw cotton bolls and wild cocoons are hand-spun on wooden charkhas. The loose yarn is immersed in warm rice water to fortify tensile strength before winding onto wooden pirns.

Phase 02

Loom Warp &
Shuttle Dance

Thousands of warp threads are manually threaded through heddles over 3 painstaking days. The artisan alternates foot treadles while flying bamboo shuttles carry the colored weft across.

Phase 03

Sun Wash &
Heirloom Finish

The woven lengths are washed in flowing river currents to release excess starch, sundried on stone riverbanks, and hand-finished with knotted fringes and GI authentic certification tags.
